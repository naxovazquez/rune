- A matched flight onto or off a zoomed or panned surface (`El::live_scale`, `El::live_offset`, `El::scale` above it, like a canvas) leaves where its source was drawn and lands where it rests. Before, a flight home into a board that mounted with it flew to its unzoomed layout and jumped once it landed, and a flight off a zoomed board left from its unzoomed layout. A flight now comes under its destination's zoom and live offsets as it travels, including a zoom an app is still driving, and starts from its source's drawn rect (`Scene::match_geometry_scaled`). Launches are unchanged. `examples/matched_zoom` and the `matched-zoom` motion-audit scenario show it.
